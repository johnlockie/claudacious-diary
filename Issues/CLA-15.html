Please create a new theme for the blog. This is "Space Theme". Should be dark, starry, with neon colors, very "spacey". Use your own judgment on the style, I just want something 'space like' please.

What was done

Added a fifth theme, Space, to the theme switcher in the top-right corner, next to Hand-written, Typewriter, Doogie Howser and Hot Dog Stand. It works like the other themes: one click switches to it, and the choice is remembered on the next visit.

Part of the pageSpace theme look
Background Near-black deep-space navy with a starfield made of two layers of stars. The brighter layer slowly twinkles. Three soft nebula clouds sit behind it: violet top-right, magenta on the left, cyan at the bottom. The sky stays fixed while the posts scroll over it.
Title ("Dear Diary,") Orbitron, a sci-fi display font, in capitals, with a cyan → violet → magenta neon gradient and a glow.
"Private" stamp A glowing neon-magenta capsule.
Each diary entry A dark, slightly see-through glass panel with rounded corners and a cyan/violet neon edge glow. The washi-tape strip is replaced by a neon gradient badge that reads ✦ CAPTAIN’S LOG ✦. The tilt is off.
Text Body text is in Exo 2, a clean, slightly futuristic sans-serif, in pale lavender. Titles are glowing cyan Orbitron, dates are small capitals in Orbitron, quotes have a magenta highlight, and the mood line is neon magenta.
"Read this to me" controls Pill-shaped neon-cyan buttons that glow brighter on hover or while reading. The paragraph being read gets a magenta highlight.

Palette: space #05060f   neon cyan #3ff4ff   violet #8a5cff   neon magenta #ff3df0   text #e4e6ff

What was changed

  • index.html:
    • A new :root[data-theme="space"] colour/font block, using the same variables as the other themes.
    • A "Space layout" CSS section: the starfield and nebula (two fixed background layers, all CSS, no images), the twinkle animation, the title, stamp, glass panels, Captain’s log badge, headings, quotes, mood line, footer and a mobile size tweak.
    • Space styles for the "Read this to me" button and voice picker.
    • A Space button in the theme switcher, styled in cyan Orbitron.
    • Added "space" to the saved-theme check at the top of the page, so the theme loads straight away on a return visit with no flash of the default theme.
    • Added the Orbitron and Exo 2 fonts to the existing Google Fonts request.
  • Added this record, Issues/CLA-15.html.
  • No other theme, entry text or script behaviour was changed. Hand-written is still the default theme.

Accessibility

  • Text is pale lavender on a near-black panel, about 15.6:1, well above the WCAG AA level of 4.5:1. The dimmer date and mood text (#9aa2d9) is about 7.8:1, and the cyan headings are about 14:1.
  • The twinkle animation turns off for readers who have "reduce motion" set on their device, the same way the Doogie Howser cursor does.
  • The stars are drawn behind the page and ignore clicks, so they never get in the way of reading or selecting text.
